Dr Shi is leading a multidisciplinary research project together with his collaborator, aiming to develop an wearable sensor system for assessing food and energy intake. The project is actively seeking enthusiastic students who wish to contribute and become part of this project. Prospective students interested in joining are invited to contact Dr. Shi directly via email. Dr. Shi extends gratitude for the generous support to this project provided by the Dame Julia Higgins Postdoc Collaborative Research Fund.
