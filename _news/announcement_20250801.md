> I’m delighted to have had the opportunity to give a talk in Professor Mauricio Villarroel’s group on “AI-Powered Multimodal Sensing: Behind and Beyond Data.” We discussed what lies behind and beyond data: the trends of sensing technologies, their interaction with machine learning, and how sensors and AI together can make a meaningful impact on human health and a human-robot co-existing future.
> Many thanks to Mauro and his team for the warm welcome and inspiring discussions!
